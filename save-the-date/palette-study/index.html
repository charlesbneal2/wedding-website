01 / The color conversation

Not a match.
A shared language.

03 / A familiar little detail

Fruit, as a whisper.
Not the whole story.

A mangosteen cross-section becomes a quiet print border. Its rounded segments echo the original illustration, without turning every corner into a decoration.

The richer fruit mark belongs on the invitation. The lighter linework belongs at the edges.

A little rhythm, a lot of breathing room.
